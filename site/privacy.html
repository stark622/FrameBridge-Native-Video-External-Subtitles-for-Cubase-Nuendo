FrameBridge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in connection with the FrameBridge website, purchase process and customer support.

Website

The FrameBridge website (framebridge.cc) is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you visit it, Cloudflare processes technical connection data (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy terms. The FrameBridge website uses no advertising or third-party tracking and sets no cookies of its own; Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies. The website keeps anonymous visit statistics on Cloudflare: pages viewed, clicks on site controls (such as Buy, the video, FAQ items and the language menu), time on the page and scroll depth, operating system, browser and device type, country, and the referring website. No IP address, cookie or persistent identifier is stored; a random number kept in your browser’s session storage only links the events of one visit and is deleted when you close the tab. Browsers that send Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track are not counted. The website shows its pages in the language of your browser settings; if you choose a language yourself, that choice is saved only in your browser’s local storage and is never sent to us.

The demo video on the home page is embedded from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only after you press play; YouTube then processes information under its own privacy terms. Links to YouTube and GitHub open those external services, which process information under their own privacy terms.

Purchases and payment processing

Purchases are processed through Creem as the merchant of record. Creem handles checkout, payment processing and related billing information under its own privacy terms. The FrameBridge operator may receive order and customer contact details that Creem makes available for fulfilment, customer support and handling purchase-related requests.

Do not send payment card details to FrameBridge support.

Support requests

If you contact support, the information you provide (such as your email address, message and any files you choose to share) may be used to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Avoid sending passwords, identity documents or unrelated personal information.

Purposes and retention

Information received through orders or support is used for order fulfilment, responding to customers, resolving technical problems and meeting applicable legal obligations. It is kept only for as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law.

The software

FrameBridge runs locally on your computer. It does not send usage data, telemetry or your media files anywhere. Subtitle text recognition uses the OCR built into Windows on your own computer.

Third-party services

Cloudflare, Creem, GitHub and YouTube have their own data-processing practices and privacy notices. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

Your requests

For questions about information handled by FrameBridge support, or requests to access, correct or delete information where applicable, contact stark622@ukr.net. Requests concerning checkout or payment information held by Creem may also need to be directed to Creem.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the product's sales, support or data-handling practices change. The current version is published at https://framebridge.cc/privacy.
