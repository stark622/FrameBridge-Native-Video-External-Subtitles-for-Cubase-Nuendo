# FrameBridge Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 October 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in connection with the FrameBridge product page, purchase process and customer support.

## Product page

The public FrameBridge product page is hosted on GitHub. When you visit that page, GitHub processes information under its own privacy terms. FrameBridge does not operate GitHub's infrastructure.

## Purchases and payment processing

Purchases, when available, are processed through Creem as the merchant of record. Creem handles checkout, payment processing and related billing information under its own privacy terms. The FrameBridge operator may receive order and customer contact details that Creem makes available for fulfilment, customer support and handling purchase-related requests.

Do not send payment card details to FrameBridge support.

## Support requests

If you contact support, the information you provide (such as your email address, message and any files you choose to share) may be used to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Avoid sending passwords, identity documents or unrelated personal information.

## Purposes and retention

Information received through orders or support is used for order fulfilment, responding to customers, resolving technical problems and meeting applicable legal obligations. It is kept only for as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law.

## Third-party services

GitHub and Creem have their own data-processing practices and privacy notices. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

## Your requests

For questions about information handled by FrameBridge support, or requests to access, correct or delete information where applicable, contact **stark622@ukr.net**. Requests concerning checkout or payment information held by Creem may also need to be directed to Creem.

## Changes

This notice may be updated if the product's sales, support or data-handling practices change. The current version will be published in this repository.
