These are specifically tested builds, not a claim for every release in the
Cubase 8-16 or Nuendo 10-15 generations. Cubase 16.0.5 passed the user's full
video, subtitle, audio and thumbnail test collection. Cubase 9 passed the full
video and subtitle collection; Cubase 8 passed three previously unsupported video
formats. Cubase Pro 12.0.51 was independently verified on a weak Windows 11 PC:
installation, MKV playback with external subtitles, and restoration by uninstall.
This does not mean all heavy media play smoothly on that PC.

Nearby or older unlisted Cubase/Nuendo builds may also be compatible: the
installer offers them only when its compatibility check of the installed video
engine passes. This includes possible Nuendo 7-9, 11 and 16 builds, but they have
not been tested and are not guaranteed. A failed check leaves the installation
untouched.

All verified hosts share the same FFmpeg decoding and subtitle rendering.
One observed UI difference is that Nuendo 13 can open its full-screen video
window about 300 ms more slowly, without affecting playback.

VERIFIED VIDEO MATRIX
---------------------

Container coverage:
  MKV / WebM, MK3D, MOV / MP4, M4V, MXF (including Avid OP-Atom), AVI, DivX,
  M2TS / MTS / TS, TOD, MOD, EVO, MPEG-PS / VOB, MPEG-1, DV, FLV, F4V,
  WMV / ASF, WTV / DVR-MS, 3GP / 3G2, RM / RMVB, OGV and OGM.

Codec coverage:
  H.264 Main/High10/B-frames/VFR, HEVC Main/Main10, AV1 8-bit/10-bit,
  ProRes Proxy/LT/422 HQ/4444, DNxHR/DNxHD, CineForm, XDCAM, XAVC/AVC-Intra,
  DV, DVCPRO50, DVCPRO HD, MPEG-1, MPEG-2, VP8, VP9 8-bit/10-bit, APV, FFV1,
  HuffYUV/FFVHuff, UtVideo, MagicYUV, Uncompressed v210, QuickTime Animation,
  PNG, TIFF, Canopus HQ/HQX, SpeedHQ, HAP/HAP Q, Theora, WMV 7/8, Xvid,
  DivX/MS MPEG-4, Sorenson Spark, Sorenson Video, RealVideo, Microsoft Video 1,
  MJPEG, H.263, Avid 1:1 and Cinepak.

Video with non-square pixels (DV, DVCPRO50, DVCPRO HD) is shown in its correct
wide proportions. Small videos (below 720 lines) that have a subtitle file are
shown enlarged to 720 lines so that the subtitles stay sharp.

LIKELY TO PLAY TOO (opens; smooth playback not guaranteed):
  VVC/H.266, HEVC 4:2:2 and 4:4:4, JPEG 2000, Lagarith, Canopus Lossless,
  WMV 9, VC-1, NotchLC, uncompressed r210/R10k/2vuy; not tested: Avid Meridian,
  SheerVideo, Apple Intermediate, VP6, TechSmith/Camtasia, Fraps, CamStudio,
  Indeo.

FFmpeg contains more decoders than the file extensions Cubase/Nuendo accept:
a file whose extension is not registered by FrameBridge cannot be imported,
whatever codec it holds.

Frame rates are reported exactly (24, 25, 30, 48, 50, 60 and the NTSC rates), so
the host shows no false frame-rate mismatch. Video above 60 fps is delivered at
60 fps. HDR-to-SDR conversion was visually verified; no quantitative
color-accuracy claim is made.

SUBTITLES
---------

Supported external subtitle formats:
  SRT, ASS, SSA, VTT, SMI/SAMI, MicroDVD, TTML, DFXP, IMSC, EBU STL, SCC,
  DCP XML, PGS/SUP and VobSub.

Dubbing scripts and dialogue lists with timecodes and roles:
  PDF, DOCX, TXT, XLSX and CSV (named like the video; shown in the SRT look
  with the role as a prefix; an ordinary subtitle file takes priority).

Text and bitmap subtitle paths are active across the verified video formats.
All formats share the SRT look. PGS/SUP and VobSub are read as text with the
Windows OCR (test SUP against its SRT: 73/76 lines identical, the rest differ by
spacing/one dot; italics 560/561 words; a full Blu-ray episode: 792 of 795 cues as
text in 3.2 s). Unreadable lines and languages without a Windows OCR pack keep the
original pictures. Supported sidecars are loaded automatically, so they do not
need to be burned into the source video in advance.

SOUND ENGINEER COPY WINDOW
--------------------------

A tray icon opens a live copy of the host's own Video Player, drawn by Windows
(Desktop Window Manager): the same frame at the same moment, with subtitles, no
second decoding and no separate playback. Verified on Cubase 8-15 and Nuendo 10.
The copy is as sharp as the Video Player it copies: show the actor the Video Player
itself in full screen and use the copy for the sound engineer. Windows 7 needs Aero.

WORKFLOW
--------

Supported files use the native Cubase/Nuendo video track and built-in Video
Player. They do not require preliminary transcoding or a separate external
video player.

PERFORMANCE NOTES
-----------------

CineForm and FFV1 use the verified first-pass seek optimization. AV1 keeps the
verified preroll path. Heavy intra, AV1, VP9 10-bit, ProRes 4444 and other
software-decoded media can be difficult on low-end hardware. On the independent
Cubase 12.0.51 test PC (i5-2550K / GTX 660), 4K HEVC Main10 and H.264 High10
were too demanding for smooth playback; audio could continue while video froze.
For weak PCs, disabling video thumbnails in the host is recommended where
available. FrameBridge automatically pauses thumbnail generation under sustained
player delay to reduce CPU contention; this does not make unsupported hardware
decode 4K 10-bit video in real time. No Vulkan dependency is required.

INSTALLER SAFETY
----------------

Automatic discovery is limited to Windows App Paths, Windows uninstall records,
and product state for rollback/status. There is no whole-drive, Program Files,
Desktop, archive, or unrelated test-copy scan.

Multiple compatible hosts require an explicit numbered selection for install and
rollback. With no compatible registered host, the standard Windows executable or
folder picker is offered. Cancelling performs no file or registry changes.

Original engines are matched by exact SHA-256 identity or by the compatibility
check, backed up per installation, bound to product state, and restored by atomic
replacement. On Cubase 16 the original AVI support module is backed up and restored
the same way. For Cubase 8/9 only, the installer also makes checked local copies
of the customer's existing QuickTime helper EXEs alongside the backed-up engine;
its own copies are removed on rollback. These EXEs are not distributed in this
package. Existing unknown FFmpeg DLLs or unknown hashes cause install refusal;
rollback removes only known product hashes. Product cleanup is limited to
%ProgramData%\CubaseNuendoDirectShow and exact extension values previously recorded
by this product. Projects, preferences, plug-ins and unrelated data are outside
the installer scope.

Recommended order when updating Cubase/Nuendo: UNINSTALL.cmd, the Steinberg
update, then INSTALL.cmd.
